# RevMeets — Social Launch Kit

Everything you need to stand up the three launch accounts. **You** do the
sign-up + verification clicks (email/SMS/CAPTCHA); the images and copy below
are ready to paste.

- **Email to use:** `revmeets@gmail.com`
- **Website:** https://revmeets.co.uk
- **Brand colour:** orange `#FF5F1F` on near-black `#0b0b0b`

## The three networks (in setup order)

1. **Instagram** — the home of UK car culture (meets, builds, photography).
2. **TikTok** — where car clips go viral; younger enthusiast audience.
3. **Facebook Page** — UK car clubs & local meets run on Facebook Events/Groups.

## Setup checklist

For each platform, in order:

- [ ] Sign in to / create the account with `revmeets@gmail.com`
- [ ] Complete email + phone verification (you'll need the Gmail inbox + a phone)
- [ ] Set the **handle** (see priority list) — same on all three
- [ ] Upload `avatar.png` as the profile picture
- [ ] (Facebook) Upload `facebook-cover.png` as the cover
- [ ] Paste the **name** and **bio** for that platform
- [ ] Add the website link `https://revmeets.co.uk`
- [ ] Switch to a **Business/Creator** account (Instagram & TikTok) — unlocks
      insights and the link in bio
- [ ] Post the first graphic and **pin it**

### After the three are up
- [ ] Add the real social URLs to the site footer (currently placeholder icons)
- [ ] Consider a **Facebook Group** (not just a Page) — UK car communities live
      in Groups; great for engagement and event cross-posting
- [ ] Cross-link: put the IG/TikTok/FB handles in each other's bios where there's room

---

## Notes

- Make the Facebook presence a **Page** (public, followable, has a cover) — not
  a personal profile. A Page is what the footer links to and what can run Events.
- Instagram & TikTok crop avatars to a **circle** — `avatar.png` already keeps
  the mark clear of the edges, so it won't get cut.
- Keep posting cadence realistic for launch: 2–3 posts/week of real event
  photos/clips beats a burst then silence.
